SMS Consent & Messaging Program

Central Land Consulting, LLC · Canton, Ohio · info@centrallandconsulting.com

Who receives our text messages

Central Land Consulting sends text messages only to landowners along the Midship Pipeline right-of-way who are clients of Central Land Consulting. Each landowner provided their mobile phone number in their signed consulting agreement with Central Land Consulting and agreed that project updates would be sent to them by text message. We do not purchase, rent, or share phone number lists, and we do not text anyone who has not provided their number to us in this way.

How consent was obtained

Opt-in method: Signed written consulting agreement. The landowner provides their mobile number in the agreement and agrees to receive project updates by text message. Consent may also be confirmed in writing through our SMS Consent Addendum. Consent is not collected by texting a keyword.

What we send

Message type: Non-promotional right-of-way project updates, such as field inspection notices, restoration status, and project notices.

Frequency: Low volume; only when there is a project update to share.

Sample message:
“Landowners, we will be in the field on your property this week performing inspections and assessing that restoration has been completed. - Central Land Consulting. Reply STOP to opt out.”

We do not send marketing, advertising, or promotional messages.

Opting out and getting help

Every message identifies Central Land Consulting and includes opt-out instructions. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP, or email info@centrallandconsulting.com, for assistance. We honor opt-out requests promptly and will not text a number again after a STOP request.

Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies.

Your information

Phone numbers are used only to send the project updates described above and are never sold or shared with third parties for marketing. See our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.
