Privacy Policy

Central Land Consulting, LLC · Effective September 23, 2026

Scope

This policy explains how Central Land Consulting, LLC ("we," "us") handles personal information of landowners on pipeline and right-of-way projects, including our text messaging program.

Information we collect

Name, mailing address, mobile phone number, email address, tract and property details, project and restoration/compliance records, and text message history.

How we use it

To carry out our consulting services; to send project and account text messages such as restoration updates and inspection or meeting notices; to answer your questions; and to keep records we are required to keep.

Sharing

We share information only as needed to do our work and operate our messaging, including with our text messaging provider and, where required, attorneys, regulators, or the project operator. We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data. Mobile numbers and consent information are not shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

Retention

We keep information while we work with you and as needed for legal, contractual, and record-keeping obligations, then delete or de-identify it.

Security

We use access controls, password-protected systems, and reputable service providers. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information by emailing info@centrallandconsulting.com. You can stop text messages at any time by replying STOP. Opting out of texts does not end your consulting agreement.

Changes

We will post updates on this page with a new effective date.

See also our Terms & Conditions and SMS Consent & Messaging Program.
